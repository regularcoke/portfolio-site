hugo a. marin (b. 2001) is a mexican multidisciplinary artist-designer creating work across physical and virtual spaces. their work is critical of internet culture and the performance of identities. intimacy and community are central to marin's artistic practice, while essentialist and spartan design choices define their design and typographic work. they are currently based in los angeles, ca.
