Hugo Adrian Marin

Hugo Adrian Marin (b. 2001) is a Mexican multidisciplinary artist-designer working across physical and virtual spaces. Intimacy, community, and queerness are central to Marin's artistic practice, while essentialist and spartan design choices define their design and typographic work. They are currently based in Los Angeles, CA. #TIME2HAVEBOTH
